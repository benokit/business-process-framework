# Project goals

- **G1 — Business domain.** A framework especially suited to business software: entities, processes, users, security, rules, services, validations.
- **G2 — Building blocks.** Efficient construction of domain abstractions (building blocks).
- **G3 — Composition.** Above all, efficient composition of those building blocks.
- **G4 — Depth.** Flexible enough to build arbitrarily deep hierarchies of abstractions.
- **G5 — Simplicity.** Everything stays simple and comprehensible, at any depth. Primary goal.
- **G6 — Robustness.** Primary goal, together with G5.
- **G7 — Minimal foundation.** A sound, minimal foundation. All business-specific concepts (G1) are built from its fundamental primitives, not added to the core.
- **G8 — Authoring by machines and humans.** Code is easy for a machine, an AI agent above all, to read, produce, change and check quickly and with little context, and stays comprehensible to the humans who review it.
